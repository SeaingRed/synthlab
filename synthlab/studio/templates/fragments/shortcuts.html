Typing rows

The number, Q, A and Z rows are each mapped to a surface — piano, notes, chords, a chord family, harmonics, or off. The keyboard dock's toolbar on Synths shows them (its QWERTY group: 1 chords · Q piano …); click that strip to change them. The choice is remembered.

Turn QWERTY playing off entirely with its typing switch, beside the strip (also in the Loops page's head).

Each key plays a note, or the chord in key built on it — one click in the dock's Play group: ` switches note / chord, Alt + ` the next chord (triad, 7th, 9th, power, octave).
