{# synthlab/studio/templates/fragments/sampler_panel.html #} {# The Source panel's sampler block (region `sampler-panel`, `samplerview.context`): the sample's waveform with its start/end, loop and slice markers (server-drawn SVG), Resample, and the chops — each slice sits on a key from the root up (wrapping), with its own pitch, reverse and a start nudge. Present only while an oscillator is "sampler" (hidden otherwise, so a pill click that turns the sampler on brings it back out of band). Buttons only, no inputs: the #patch form posts on every `input` inside it. Each slice's start is also a marker over the waveform (`.slice-mark`, role=slider): layout.js drags it or nudges it with the arrows and posts `action=move` with its position. The detected pitch (`samplerview.pitch_context`) has a "use" that makes it the root. ● Record sample (static/samplerec.js) counts a bar in at the session's bpm and records 1, 2 or 4 bars from the mic into the sampler (`/fragment/sampler/record`); its length and click are buttons too, remembered per browser and set again after every redraw. With a zone map (a multi-sample instrument) the map and its rows (fragments/sampler_zones.html) stand in for the waveform, the pitch and the chops; "＋ zone" opens the clip library, whose rows add one. #}
