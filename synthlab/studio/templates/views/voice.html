Voice

Make the synth talk — your voice shapes its sound.

Record a voice, drop one in or take a clip from the library, then Vocode it: the take supplies the words, the carrier — your sound, the loop or another clip — supplies the tone. Keep what you like as a clip. The Master FX are applied to the result; a layer’s FX are part of the loop, so they reach the vocoder only through “the Synths loop” carrier.
