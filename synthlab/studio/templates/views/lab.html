Lab

The Sample Lab: bring in a song, split it into stems, cut out the parts you want.

1. Import a recording and check its tempo and bar 1 (the ruler numbers the bars). 2. Separate splits it into drums, bass, vocals and the rest, a lane each. 3. Drag on a lane to select a sample of THAT lane (the mix lane: the recording; Shift adds a stem) — it snaps to bars, or to beats (Alt held = free); drag its edges on the ruler; click a lane to hear it from there. Ctrl + wheel zooms in round the pointer, Shift + wheel scrolls, the box on the strip at the top is what you see. 4. Space plays the selection round and round; ← → move it a beat (Shift a bar), [ ] make it shorter or longer, Enter keeps it as a clip named for what it is and where. ⤷ Sampler, ✂ Chop and → Beat are one press too; the cards beside make a voice, vocal chops, a pattern, a kit or a take; the line under the selection opens what was made, or undoes it. Right-click a lane, a chop or a song for its menu. Whether you may sample or separate a recording you did not make is for you to check; the Lab does not.
