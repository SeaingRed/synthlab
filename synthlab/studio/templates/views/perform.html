Loops

Make and play ideas: the launcher's slots and scenes over the editor, the selected lane beside them.

Drag loops from the Loops panel (Alt+O) onto the launcher's slots. A click on a slot selects it — the editor opens its lane, Notes or Drums as the slot is — and launches it on the next bar (Alt+1…8 a scene, Alt+0 stops all); the slot that plays saves your edits to its loop. The lane inspector at the right holds the selected lane's sound, layers, level and FX, and ✦ Coach (Alt+K). Play holds the pads (Q W E R / A S D F with the keyboard dock's typing → pads; Alt+P), the drum XY, the performance FX and the beats.
