Loops

Make and play ideas: the launcher's slots and scenes over the editor, the selected lane beside them.

The deck at the top is the page's one transport: ▶ (Space) plays its source — Loop (the selected cell's loop alone), Scene (its row) or Song — and ⏺ Rec records, its ▾ saying how long and where the take lands. Drag loops from the Loops panel (Alt+O) onto the launcher's cells; double-click one (or Enter) to edit it, right-click it for the rest. A click on a cell selects it: its loop opens in the editor on its lane (Notes or Drums as the cell is) and plays there from the next bar, and every edit saves to it — an empty cell's lane is a sketch until "Save as loop" (Alt+1…8 launch a scene, Alt+0 stops every lane). The lane inspector at the right holds the selected lane's sound, layers, level and FX, and ✦ Coach (Alt+K). Pads ▴ in the keyboard dock's Tools opens Play: the pads (Q W E R / A S D F with the keyboard dock's typing → pads; Alt+P), the drum XY, the performance FX and the beats. The editors' Tools ▾ hold the pattern tools a click away.
