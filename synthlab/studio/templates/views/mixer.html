Mix

One strip a track: level, pan, two sends, a duck under the kick, a three-band EQ. Returns and the master on the right.

Drag a fader or a knob (Shift, or a pen’s barrel button: fine), or focus it and use the wheel or the arrow keys; double-click or the eraser is its default; click the value to type one. Tab moves control to control; a knob’s or a fader’s own arrows change it (← → pan a pan knob). A and B send a track to the shared reverb and delay — the first send turned up adds them. Balance sets each track that has a role to its level under the drums. Every change is one Ctrl+Z, and is heard from the next pass.
