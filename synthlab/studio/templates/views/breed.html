Breed

Steer generations of offspring toward a sound you love.

Tap a card to hear the sound on its own; ⟳ loops it in your mix, exactly as it will sound on Synths; ★ makes it a parent of the next generation; Use on Synths takes it there. With depth set to layered or deep a card is a whole sound — layers that play the main lane's notes, effects, curves — and Use offers it with its layers or the main sound alone. The meters on a card are measured on one A3 note: sub weight and the spectral centre. Right-click a card for its menu.
