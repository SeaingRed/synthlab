Clips

Every clip and sample: listen, tune, send it anywhere, make a beat or a part from it.

Pick a clip on the left to open it on the bench. ▶ plays it (⟲ loops it; click the waveform to jump). Tune pitches, trims, reverses and speeds it up or down — heard at once, kept only when you press Keep (a new clip) or Update (written over this one: ↶ Undo puts it back). Send to puts it on a drum row, in the song, on Synths as a sampler, in the Voice lab or the Sample Lab. Make from it listens to the clip and writes a beat on Kit or a part on Perform's note grid, played by the kit or sound you pick — Preview first, then Apply; ↶ Undo takes an Apply back whole. ✦ Describe asks Gemini to choose for you: it hears the first seconds of the clip.
