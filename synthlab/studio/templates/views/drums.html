Kit

The drum kit: eight voices, each synthesised or a sample — shape them here; your beats live in the Loops.

Tap a pad to hear a voice on its own; drag its knobs to shape it — the loop follows. The grid is the loop's beat: edit it here or on the Loops page, Save beat keeps it in the Loops panel, and Beats ▸ opens one (the drawer, with the bank's old slots).
